# Twitch Chat Translator

Translate Twitch chat and your replies into your chosen language.

## [Install Twitch Translator →](https://shigexdw.github.io/Twitch-Chat-Auto-Translator/)

Follow the three steps on the install page. Requires Tampermonkey on a desktop browser.

After installing, refresh Twitch and click **⚙ beside the chatbox** to choose your language.

Text is sent to your selected translation provider. AI and DeepL may require an API key and incur charges.
